# Signalpost: semantic evaluation requirements

Checked 17 October 2026 against the live official documents, retrieved without external keys. Preserved responses, SHA-256 values and reference fixtures are under `out/semantic-verification/requirements/`. This document defines a local evidence review; it does not supply an official score or claim an exhaustive internet gold set.

## Which rubric governs

The current evaluation contract allocates **50 recall/coverage, 30 precision/identity/evidence, 12 synthesis and 8 UX**. For an external field family, recall is **0.7 × company recall + 0.3 × individual-fact recall**, measured against the independently checked, deduplicated union contributed by entrants and Builderr. All entrants are rescored against the same versioned union. The contract does **not** publish exact weights between external families or a complete field ontology. Do not invent either. The 65/100 qualification threshold applies to an official run, not local smoke tests. [Evaluation contract](https://builderr.ai/docs/signalpost-evaluation-harness.md)

The live board reviewed on 17 October describes a provisional 300-company round. Its asterisk says unavailable review/rating/sentiment evidence was excluded, and measured 40-point recall was scaled to 50. This is an explicit limitation of that round, not permission to assume those opportunities never exist. The detailed contract still describes an official shared batch of 1,000 that may grow to 1,100. These are different contexts; do not replace the official fresh-batch requirement with the provisional board size. [Live challenge](https://builderr.ai/challenges/signalpost)

The public starter contains `score_competition_v3.py` with 55/15/10/12/8 proxy weights, `score_company_completeness.py` with another completeness rubric, and `evaluate_external_footprint.py` with 0.995/0.98 local precision gates. Their own descriptions identify optimization proxies. **None supersedes the current 50/30/12/8 contract or creates extra official qualification thresholds.** [Official starter archive](https://builderr.ai/signalpost-starter-kit.tar.gz), [evaluation contract](https://builderr.ai/docs/signalpost-evaluation-harness.md)

## Required meaning, not just populated fields

The participant brief requires seven envelope sections. The following local atomization makes them reviewable; it is an operational interpretation where the official brief does not prescribe individual JSON field names. [Participant brief](https://builderr.ai/starter-briefs/signalpost.md)

| Section | What counts as a supported fact | What must remain distinct |
|---|---|---|
| Legal identity/public brand | Exact organization number, registered legal name/form, registered address/industry and supported brand-to-entity relationship | A similar name, a parent brand, a franchise or the current owner does not prove the requested legal entity |
| Annual accounts/history | Each actual filed metric with value, scale, currency, reporting year and company/group scope; filed years and source availability | Missing, omitted, zero, consolidated group and entity accounts; current-year claims versus latest filed year |
| Leadership/workplaces | Each named official person-role relationship and registered subunit tied to the exact entity | Registered roles versus public operational titles; registered/postal address versus staffed operating office |
| Website/company-owned profiles | Exact candidate-to-entity binding, canonical site/profile URL and stated ownership scope | An official outbound account link proves a link relationship, not unseen account posts, followers or current employment counts |
| Hiring | Each identifiable vacancy, employer/subunit binding, dates/status and source; separately stated recruitment policies | Active vacancy, expired advert, open application, fully staffed statement and source-scoped zero matches |
| Dated public activity | A specific event/announcement, correct entity, event date and durable permitted evidence | Publication/event/retrieval date; old event versus recent activity; company promotion versus independent sentiment |
| Evidence/refresh | Snapshot-bound support, reporting/effective dates, prior values and evidence-backed material changes | Source change versus new extraction, failed refresh versus actual disappearance |

Official registers anchor identity but do not establish a public brand or operating site by themselves. Search results are candidate generators; publish from the underlying preserved source. Group/parent/subsidiary scopes must stay labelled. Promotional copy cannot serve as independent sentiment. Restricted-platform access remains restricted even when a free client exists. [Source policy](https://builderr.ai/starter-briefs/signalpost-sources.md)

## Denominators and unavailable evidence

For family `f`, let `G_f` be the deduplicated independently verified positive facts in the reference pool at the declared cutoff, `C_f` the companies with at least one such fact, and `TP_f` the matching supported entrant facts. Then:

```
company_recall_f = |companies(TP_f)| / |C_f|
fact_recall_f    = |TP_f| / |G_f|
family_recall_f  = .7 * company_recall_f + .3 * fact_recall_f
claim_precision = supported_exact_entity_published_atoms / all_published_atoms
```

An exact excerpt may contain several facts and a JSON object may contain many roles, jobs or financial values. Split these into atoms before scoring; one description field cannot stand in for all facts on a site. Deduplicate the same job/event across URLs and snapshots. Report entity precision separately from claim accuracy and count material wrong-company publications explicitly. Do not score unresolved labels as true. If a denominator is zero, report it as unmeasured rather than perfect. The official sparse-pool rule is fewer than 15 positive company-field opportunities across at least three external families; local family-level missing denominators must also be visible. [Evaluation contract](https://builderr.ai/docs/signalpost-evaluation-harness.md)

`not_available` means checked absence under a declared search/source scope, not absence from the entire internet. `blocked`, `ambiguous`, `failed` and `not_applicable` are different outcomes. Abstention cannot earn coverage. For opportunity discovery, a zero NAV match is not evidence of no hiring, and a company without a verified site is not automatically a company without a public site. [Minimal starter output contract](https://builderr.ai/signalpost-starter-kit.tar.gz), [source policy](https://builderr.ai/starter-briefs/signalpost-sources.md)

## All-1,000 local semantic protocol

1. **Freeze the original run.** Preserve original inputs, ordered JSONL, code/policy/model versions, snapshots, timing/cost receipts and NAV cutoff. Give all subsequent research and corrections distinct run IDs. These 1,000 become a development/evaluation corpus after fixes; they cannot remain a blind final holdout.
2. **Inventory every published atom.** Expand financial objects, role arrays, workplace arrays, NAV adverts and external text claims. Retain the original parent claim ID, evidence IDs, exact entity, unit/year/scope, source hash and extraction version. Review every published website binding and external atom for exact entity, semantic entailment, qualification/date and source rights. Check official JSON values independently of normalized output; inspect omitted financial metrics and annual-account histories as recall opportunities too.
3. **Build reference positives independently for every company.** Give the discovery reviewer only the organization number and authoritative registry anchor, not the entrant's accepted claims or its rejection reasons. Use the permitted source ladder: public registry identifiers/site seeds; independent native-search query plans for legal name, brand, verified leaders and registered locations; official sitemaps, language roots, about/contact/location/careers/news pages; public annual-account copies and relevant structured data. Save all candidate and rejection receipts, search depth and failures. Search all six current external families even when the entrant found nothing. Existing Codex subscription discovery is allowed by the user's constraint; no Exa, Firecrawl, external bearer tokens or private keys.
4. **Separate discovery from adjudication.** Discovery creates candidate positives, never automatic gold. A second semantic pass receives source bytes and registry identity and assigns `supported_exact`, `wrong_entity`, `unsupported`, `outdated_for_claim`, `wrong_scope`, `inconclusive` or `source_blocked`. Store the source span and a short rationale. Labels produced by models remain **assisted semantic labels**, including when a different prompt/model is used. A model cannot certify its own independence merely by returning a confidence score.
5. **Compare both directions.** Join supported independent positives against published atoms to identify false negatives, and published atoms against independent support to find false positives. Review all disagreements and all high-risk bindings (groups/franchises/holdings/shared hosts, location scope, vacancy status, negative claims and financial unit/year/scope). A source with no independently found positive remains `unknown opportunity` unless checked absence is justified. Report the number of companies/families whose opportunity status remains unresolved.
6. **Review saturation honestly.** For unresolved opportunities, use a second discovery route that does not simply replay the same name query: verified leader/brand bridge, sitemap/language-root traversal, official structured data and public PDF text. Record marginal new supported facts by discovery pass. Decreasing yield is evidence about the bounded search process, not proof of web exhaustiveness. Overlap/capture-recapture estimates can be exploratory diagnostics, never official recall, because discovery routes are correlated and heterogeneous.
7. **Fix causes and re-evaluate.** Turn each confirmed error into a source-backed positive plus adversarial sibling/group/wrong-identifier fixture. Reprocess preserved bytes first; independently retrieve missed permitted sources when required. Retain original and corrected metrics, policy changes, gains/losses and incremental time/cost. Never silently rewrite the original fresh benchmark or original unknown usage.
8. **Verify refresh semantics.** Reuse an identical saved snapshot and require zero duplicate/false-change events. Use independently adjudicated changed snapshots for added/removed role, closed job, new location/filing and changed description; test a failed refresh retaining last supported values. Live re-fetches alone do not establish a true change without before/after semantic labels.
9. **Publish measured bounds.** Report per-family company/fact recall against the **assisted reference pool**, supported-claim/entity precision, material wrong-company count, evidence/date/scope validity, unresolved-label count, declared source cutoff, original versus repair cost/time and revised latency. Human adjudication of every disagreement/high-risk case and a predeclared random stratified sample is needed to calibrate assisted error; preserve sample size and uncertainty. Do not describe unreviewed all-1,000 labels as human gold. A new untouched final set is needed for an independent post-fix generalization claim.

This protocol follows the learning harness's ordering: wrong-company harm, support, evidence, then coverage, refresh and efficiency. It improves evidence beyond mechanical checks while preserving the difference between assisted review, human gold and the organizer's pooled official score. [Learning harness](https://builderr.ai/starter-briefs/signalpost-learning-harness.md), [playbook](https://builderr.ai/starter-briefs/signalpost-agent-playbook.md)

## Evidenced implementation gaps to investigate

- `src/service.ts` native candidate filtering excludes PDF/DOC links before retrieval. HTML-only discovery cannot establish recall for events or operational facts available only in permitted public PDFs. Add guarded public text-PDF support only with exact-entity checks, page evidence and bounded parser resources; do not download restricted platform exports.
- The current `public_activity` validator requires the published excerpt itself to name the exact legal entity even when a source is already accepted as a single-company host. A first-person dated announcement can therefore be rejected. Review actual source cases and distinguish verified single-company first-person pages from shared/group sites before changing this gate.
- `src/domain.ts` permits six extracted external families. There are no native proposal fields for independent reviews, ratings, sentiment or platform metrics. This is a schema limitation if permitted positive evidence is found; it is not proof that a fixed number of current official points is lost. The live provisional board excludes unavailable review/rating/sentiment, and the old starter proxy cannot supply the missing current weights.
- `src/service.ts` finalization generally marks missing external families `ambiguous` when any research was attempted. This is safer than claiming universal absence, but it does not identify whether each particular family was searched, blocked or exhausted. The semantic report needs a per-family attempt and opportunity-status ledger.

These are code-level risk findings, not verified counts of missed facts. A fix requires the actual saved/public source case and before/after semantic evidence; a rule difference alone is not a false-negative gold label.

The exact holdout has a confirmed omitted NIF description and historical event in its previously returned public ethical-trade report. Page 5 gives its full legal name and headquarters matching the official registry, and calls it an umbrella organization for Norwegian sport. Page 12 documents adoption of its sustainability strategy on 17 November 2022. Original output has both description and activity unavailable. This is a positive source case, not a claim of new/current activity. The same report's 2023 employees/revenue must not replace current registered employees or latest filed accounts. Raw PDF, page text, rendered page review, robots/HTTP/hash receipts and regression expectations are in `out/semantic-verification/requirements/pdf-nif-probe/` and `pdf-semantic-fixtures.json`.

The Brekke & Strand approval certificate supplies exact organization number 916863071 but expired on 5 November 2024; it is a negative regression against current-certification claims and against taking the regulator's footer address as a company office. The Gressvik Marina press release supplies a dated 11 April 2023 dealership-rights transaction, but no organization number; further exact-company binding is required. Nimbus's group sales, employees and spokesperson in that release cannot become Gressvik facts. The Hydro transparency link returned HTTP 404; that failed fetch is preserved. No protected-platform evidence was collected.

The complete public product-sample HTML was also checked: none of its 543 distinct nine-digit strings overlaps this holdout. `product-overlap.json` records the source hash and empty intersection. The sample cannot resolve semantic labels for these 1,000 companies.
