# Registry semantics: the exact 1,000 companies

Reviewed 17 October 2026 from every preserved official source, using a separate Python analysis rather than the application normalizers. No model calls, external keys or new 1,000-company sweep were used. This checks the meaning of the official schema and every resulting edge case, not the truth of self-reported filings outside the register. Artifacts and repeatable analysis are in `out/semantic-verification/registry/`.

## Confirmed omissions requiring correction

**Company status and registered activities were discarded.** All 1,000 identity anchors were examined. The preserved official sources contain `aktivitet` for 998 entities, `vedtektsfestetFormaal` for 948, historical names for 244, liquidation status for six, bankruptcy status for one and deletion dates for two. `identityFields` omits these. Registered activity and legal purpose can make a profile useful without a verified public site, provided they remain explicitly labelled registered/self-reported remit, not independently observed current operations. Status/deletion dates also qualify whether current leadership, operations or a website relationship should be inferred. The complete source-backed cases are in `identity-status-review.json`. [Current official schema](https://data.brreg.no/enhetsregisteret/api/dokumentasjon/no/index.html)

**Registered roles lose material status.** The 998 roles responses contain 4,080 relations. All 37 with `avregistrert=true` are excluded correctly, leaving 4,043 published registered relations. However, 12 retained relations have `person.erDoed=true`, two corporate role holders have `enhet.erSlettet=true`, and two auditors/accountants have a negative approval status. The normalizer drops those flags. Three deceased people are still shown in `Daglig leder` relations (organizations 912798917, 989855328 and 981160460). Preserve the register's relation and status; do not represent it unqualified as living current operational leadership. Do not silently erase evidence or infer a death date. Group `sistEndret` is a registry group-update date, not an individual's appointment date. Corporate accountants/auditors are corporate role holders, not executive people. Full local regression cases are in `role-edge-review.json`. [Official roles schema](https://data.brreg.no/enhetsregisteret/api/dokumentasjon/no/index.html#tag/roller)

**Liquidation accounts lose their account context.** Organization 975797805 has three company statements; the latest 2025 statement, record 7007174, explicitly has `avviklingsregnskap=true`. The current normalized record and latest-metric claims omit that flag. Preserve liquidation-account status alongside scope, period, accounting framework and audit qualifiers where given; an ordinary current operating-company comparison is misleading without it. [Official accounting API schema](https://data.brreg.no/regnskapsregisteret/regnskap/v3/api-docs/regnskapsregisteret)

## Financial selection, units and warnings

The 998 successful annual-account responses contain 2,798 statements: 2,769 `SELSKAP` and 29 `KONSERN`. The audit separately selected each scope's latest reporting end, checked the actual source organization's identifier and compared each published metric to its statement and currency. **6,728 latest financial atoms preserve their corresponding reported source value, period and scope; no mapping/selection error was found by this analysis.** Numeric source IDs are normalized as strings, which is immaterial. No exchange-rate conversion or fabricated missing-to-zero substitution was found.

Twelve statements are non-NOK: nine USD statements across organizations 930776793 and 915184367, and three EUR statements for 926593811. They remain in their stated currencies. There are 101 non-calendar/partial periods across 91 companies; the full start/end dates must be retained in comparisons, and growth must not assume every reporting period has equal length. No duplicate latest reporting ends or discrepancy between the available latest company-account year and registered latest-submitted year was found in the successful sources. `revenue` currently maps to **sumDriftsinntekter (total operating income)**; this is not necessarily sales revenue. In 475 latest published company/group observations the sales-income subfield differs or is omitted. Label the metric precisely; do not describe it as pure product sales. These definitions follow the official schema's separate `salgsinntekter` and `sumDriftsinntekter` fields. [Accounting API schema](https://data.brreg.no/regnskapsregisteret/regnskap/v3/api-docs/regnskapsregisteret)

All **36 balance warnings across 28 companies** were inspected from the raw statements. They reproduce source inconsistencies between assets, equity and liabilities; they are not evidence of a normalization arithmetic bug. `financial-warning-review.json` records every source triple, the separately reported equity/liability total, period, scope and discrepancy. Some are small rounding-like differences (2, 1,000 or 2,000 reported currency units); those cannot automatically be called rounding without the original accounting-unit notes. Others are material: organization 989193139's 2023 source reports assets 150,539 against equity 257,726 plus liabilities 52,781. Preserve reported values and prominently flag source disagreement. Do not force the balance by inventing debt/equity or replacing a source metric with a calculated one.

The API schema reports numeric amounts and currency, without a per-record scale field. The application preserves these numbers as `currency_units_as_reported`. This review verifies faithful API-level units; it does not independently compare all 2,798 records to original PDF display scales. A latest-metric comparison should therefore retain the exact source paths and unit wording rather than invent a thousands/millions multiplier.

## Missing accounts and history

Two organizations, 992878215 and 984891776, returned HTTP 500 twice on the normalized account endpoint. They correctly remain `failed`, not zero or confirmed absent. Their preserved official history lists include 2025. A failed normalized endpoint therefore does not establish that no latest filed accounts exist. An attempted guarded check of each official 2025 PDF was stopped because the robots endpoint closed the connection; no PDF content was obtained. These attempts are preserved under `missing-account-pdfs/`, and must not be described as a PDF 404 or confirmed unavailability. The documented public annual-copy API can be evaluated as an explicit official API connector with its published source policy rather than an unrecorded generic browser bypass. [Official annual-copy API](https://data.brreg.no/regnskapsregisteret/regnskap/v3/api-docs/aarsregnskap)

The key-number API describes the latest three annual accounts including consolidated figures. The older available-history endpoint enumerates documents, not historical financial values. The existing `financial_history` note correctly states that PDF contents have not been extracted. **A list of available historical years is not complete numerical financial-history recall.** Keep the current distinction visible; any financial-history expansion needs per-document year/scope/unit provenance and comparison with available filings. [Accounting API](https://data.brreg.no/regnskapsregisteret/regnskap/v3/api-docs/regnskapsregisteret), [annual-copy API](https://data.brreg.no/regnskapsregisteret/regnskap/v3/api-docs/aarsregnskap)

## Employees, workplaces and leadership absence

The current official entity documentation states that counts for businesses with zero through four employees may be null and supplies `harRegistrertAntallAnsatte` separately. Among these source anchors, 190 have a true registered-employees flag and a null/blank count; 236 subunit records have the same distinction. The application correctly does not invent an exact count or turn these into zero. Registered employee counts are source observations, not FTEs, average annual-report employees, social-profile association counts or proof of today's staffed headcount. [Official entity documentation](https://data.brreg.no/enhetsregisteret/api/dokumentasjon/no/index.html)

All 1,000 workplace responses were reviewed: 963 returned registered subunits bind to the requested parent. There were no mismatched parent IDs, truncated pagination, deleted returned subunits or postal-only address fallbacks in this corpus. This confirms registered-subunit coverage, **not** complete operating-office coverage. Location/hiring semantics still require separate company-page or vacancy evidence where claimed.

Two roles endpoints, for 933612732 and 992577762, returned HTTP 404. The current output says `not_available`; it must be understood as this endpoint result, not a factual statement that no human leads the company. A later independent role source could add a positive without contradicting the original source observation. No evidence supports creating a leader from a public name match alone.

## Review limits and artifacts

`summary.json` and `all-1000-registry-audit.jsonl` cover every organization; `edge-cases.json`, `financial-warning-review.json`, `role-edge-review.json` and `identity-status-review.json` preserve the semantic cases. The official current schema was extracted from the live documentation's embedded Redoc specification. The public GitHub `brreg/openAPI` schema was also retrieved but is version 1.0.0 and stale; it was not used to override the current v2 privacy/role fields. No official scoring reference pool or human adjudication was introduced by this registry review. External-world truth, full numerical historical-account recall and the final challenge score remain separate measurements.
