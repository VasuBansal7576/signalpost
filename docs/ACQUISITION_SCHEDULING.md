# Acquisition scheduling, 2026-10-17

Registry city, Norway and (for non-social identity queries) exact organisation number are added to discovery queries. Registered/email and verified hosts, exact-ID URLs and locality are scheduling hints; ownership/publication checks remain independent.

On unverified candidate sites, inspect legal/operator information early. Explicit operator declarations and named foreign imprints may defer commercial expansion when exact target identity is absent. Incidental third-party IDs, copyright and foreign language alone are insufficient. Compare normalized operator names, including omitted legal suffixes. Preserve one contact and up to two about/group identity probes after a mismatch, and rescind the scheduling stop when target identity appears. Saved sources remain available and later search candidates are never blacklisted. Verified sites retain the original six-family navigation plan. Unverified sites can admit at most three extra identity probes within the existing global request/page/deadline budgets.

Unavailable registered-domain seeds now emit an event rather than silently disappearing.

Validation: 35 focused offline regression tests; typecheck and macOS build. Two independent source reviews closed their findings. Saved HAAB/Bondensvalg scheduling comparisons use the same historical URL lists and exact saved readable responses, failing closed when a counterfactual response is absent. Controlled navigation requests on two wrong-company roots decrease from 6 to 2 and 2 to 1; ownership remains rejected. These counts are virtual scheduling operations, not measured network, latency or quality gains. The full saved discovery replay does not produce a new accepted owner for either company. Query changes require a separate fresh cohort to establish any recall effect.

The frozen fresh100 run uses e558e5a and does not contain this change. Do not relabel its results as validation of the new acquisition scheduler.
